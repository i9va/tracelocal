# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

tracelocal is an open project and we want it to stay that way — welcoming to contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. We're committed to keeping participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct, constructive, and kind in code reviews and discussions
- Assume good intent; ask questions before assuming malice
- Respect that people have different levels of experience and different ways of working
- Accept feedback gracefully — disagreement is fine, personal attacks are not

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Deliberate intimidation or sustained disruptive behaviour
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behaviour that violates this code of conduct, please report it by opening a confidential issue or reaching out directly to the maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, and other interactions that are not aligned with this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.
